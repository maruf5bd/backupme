             opts: --name --host --port INT --username --password --key-file
                   --path --share --remote --base-url --api-key --email
                   --refresh-token --folder --bwlimit --quota --priority INT
                   --weight INT --test/--no-test (default: --test)
             NOTE: --test/--no-test is accepted but NEVER USED in the body;
                   "Test connection now?" confirm always prompts.
